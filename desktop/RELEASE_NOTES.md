## Download StockKaro

| Your computer | Download |
| ------------- | -------- |
| Windows 10 or 11 | **StockKaro-Setup.exe** |
| Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) | **StockKaro-mac-arm64.dmg** |
| Mac with an Intel processor | **StockKaro-mac-x64.dmg** |
| Linux (any distribution) | **StockKaro-linux-x86_64.AppImage** |
| Ubuntu or Debian | **StockKaro-linux-amd64.deb** |

Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu → About This Mac: "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple silicon.

### First launch

StockKaro is not yet signed with a Microsoft or Apple developer certificate, so your computer asks you to confirm
the first time:

- **Windows**: if "Windows protected your PC" appears, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **macOS**: open the disk image and drag StockKaro to Applications. If macOS says it cannot verify the app, open
  **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to StockKaro.
- **Linux AppImage**: make the file executable (right-click → Properties → Allow executing, or
  `chmod +x StockKaro-linux-x86_64.AppImage`) and open it.

### Good to know

- Everything stays on your computer: your account, trades and settings are stored only there. The first account
  you create owns the app and can use its admin panel.
- StockKaro needs the internet for live prices (NSE/BSE via Yahoo Finance, about 15 minutes delayed). Offline it
  still opens, on simulated prices.
- Trading uses virtual money (paper trading). Market data and the chart analyzer's Buy, Wait and Avoid verdicts
  are for information and education only, not investment advice.
